# Stay Safe Online – Cybercrime Prevention Checklist

## General Safety Tips
- Never share your OTP, PIN, or passwords with anyone.
- Do not click on unknown or suspicious links.
- Always verify the identity of the person before trusting them online.
- Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts.
- Keep your apps and devices updated regularly.
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive transactions.

## UPI / Financial Safety Tips
- Never approve a "collect request" unless you are sure you are receiving money.
- Remember: you do NOT need to enter a PIN to receive money.
- Do not share your UPI PIN with anyone.
- Double-check payment details before sending money.

## Reporting Cybercrime
- Report fraud immediately at: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- Call Helpline Number: 1930
