# Social Media Do's and Don'ts

## ✅ Do's
- Use a professional profile picture and username.
- Think before posting anything online.
- Share useful and positive content.
- Respect others’ opinions and communicate politely.
- Keep your personal information private.

## ❌ Don'ts
- Do not share passwords or sensitive details.
- Avoid posting inappropriate or offensive content.
- Do not engage in cyberbullying or arguments.
- Avoid clicking on suspicious links or messages.
- Do not overshare personal life or location details.
